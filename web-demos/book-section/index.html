What changed

  1. A slow drip from the IV bag. One drop every few seconds from the cover's tube. It turns off for people who have Reduce Motion set.
  2. Pills become plain labels. They looked like buttons but did nothing. “PDF + EPUB” is new, because it's the first thing buyers of a download ask.
  3. Buy button right after the first paragraph. Before, it sat about 1,400px down. It beats softly in gold every few seconds. Tapped, it fills with the cover's plum and opens checkout.
  4. Read the first page free. Opens the first page right here, fading out into “Keep reading — $9.99”.
  5. Donation tracker. Replaces “2 introductions sold” and the old money box. It shows money raised toward a goal and fills when it scrolls into view. The numbers here are examples.
  6. The italic description moves down, under the buttons. It's good writing, but it was standing between people and the button.
  7. Reader quotes slot. Hidden until there are three real quotes, then shown here, next to the button.
  8. Buy bar at the bottom of the screen. Slides up once the gold button is scrolled past, so buying is one tap from anywhere on the page. Scroll down to see it.
