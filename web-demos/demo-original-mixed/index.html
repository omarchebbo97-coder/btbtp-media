Questions

Before you ask

Is this book medical advice?

No. I'm not a doctor or therapist. The book shares my lived experience and practical field notes. Always talk to your own care team before acting on anything you read here.

Who is this book for?

Patients on dialysis or awaiting transplant, recipients rebuilding their lives, the caregivers beside them, and anyone who loves an honest memoir about survival and identity.

Do I need medical knowledge to follow it?

Not at all. The story carries you; medical moments are explained in plain words, as I understood them from the patient's chair.
